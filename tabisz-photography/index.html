About

A considered approach to every commission

Tabisz Photography is a full-service studio based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, photographing weddings, portraits, and athletics throughout the region. The approach is consistent across every genre: honest light, careful composition, and direction that puts people at ease.

Every commission includes a planning consultation, a curated online gallery, and print-ready files. Travel throughout western Pennsylvania is included; destination work is available by arrangement.
